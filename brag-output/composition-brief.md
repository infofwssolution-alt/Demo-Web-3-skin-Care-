# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Veloura Skin AI Assistant

## Objective
Create a 75–80 second portfolio showcase video demonstrating a custom AI chatbot built for an e-commerce skincare brand, ready for an Upwork freelancer portfolio.

## Output Specifications
- Composition Directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Render Target: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 Landscape (1920x1080)
- Target Duration: 80 seconds (0:00 – 1:20)
- Framerate: 30 fps

## Source Material
- Project Root: `c:/FWS/demo web 3 (Skincare)`
- Client ID: `skincare-demo`
- Key UI Elements: Floating Veloura Skin Assistant launcher (`#7C9A82` / `#9370DB`), product cards with clean typography, responsive cart drawer.

## Creative Direction
- **Tone:** Calm, premium, product-focused.
- **Narrative:** Honest client portfolio piece showing real chat questions and accurate responses grounded in store policies.
- **Avoid:** Exaggerated conversion metrics, startup buzzwords, medical claims.

## Timeline & Scene Sequence
1. **Scene 1 (0:00 – 0:06)**: Title Card: `"AI skincare assistant: a custom chatbot demo"` with store context.
2. **Scene 2 (0:06 – 1:00)**: Primary Chat Showcase with 4 sequential captions:
   - `0:06 – 0:20`: `"Builds routines for your skin type"`
   - `0:20 – 0:34`: `"Tailors advice to someone else's skin"`
   - `0:34 – 0:47`: `"Knows which ingredients work together"`
   - `0:47 – 1:00`: `"Flags what's not right for sensitive skin"`
3. **Scene 3 (1:00 – 1:12)**: Responsive Cross-Device Montage:
   - Caption: `"Works on desktop and mobile. Answers come only from the store's own catalog and policies."`
4. **Scene 4 (1:12 – 1:20)**: End Card:
   - `"Custom AI chatbots for your online store"`
   - `[YOUR AGENCY NAME]`
   - `[YOUR CONTACT / UPWORK PROFILE]`
