# Brag Plan: Veloura Skin AI Assistant (Portfolio Demo)

## What is this project?
A working demo of a custom AI e-commerce chatbot assistant integrated into "Veloura Skin", a modern skincare store. The showcase highlights real retrieval-augmented answers grounded strictly in the store's catalog and policies.

## The Angle
An honest, unhyped portfolio piece for an AI chatbot developer targeting e-commerce store owners on Upwork. Rather than making exaggerated marketing promises, it demonstrates real customer interactions: custom routine formulation, ingredient safety checks, return policies, and transparent fallback when questions fall outside store knowledge.

## Hook (0:00 – 0:06)
A clean, editorial title card: **"AI skincare assistant: a custom chatbot demo"**, transitioning to an ambient glimpse of the Veloura Skin store hero, immediately framing this as a practical e-commerce tool.

## Key Moments & Structure (Target: ~75–80 Seconds)

1. **Scene 1: Title Card & Store Context (0:00 – 0:06, 6s)**
   - Text: `"AI skincare assistant: a custom chatbot demo"`
   - Visual: Warm minimalist cream background (`#F4F1EC`), transitioning into the live Veloura Skin store hero.
   - Purpose: Establishes context and brand aesthetics.

2. **Scene 2: Live Chat Showcase with Step Captions (0:06 – 1:00, 54s)**
   - Visual: Screen recording of the live chat widget in action (or dedicated placeholder segment).

## Tone & Creative Direction
- **Tone preset:** `polished` / `product-focused`
- **Creative direction:** Calm, premium, transparent, client-ready.
- **Pacing:** Unhurried, deliberate transitions allowing store owners to read real bot responses.
- **Restraints:** Strictly no launch hype ("introducing", "revolutionary"), no fake conversion stats, no medical claims.

## Visual Identity (From Project)
- **Background:** `#F4F1EC` (Soft warm porcelain)
- **Primary Text:** `#141615` (Deep charcoal)
- **Accent Color:** `#7C9A82` (Sage green) / `#9370DB` (Lavender accent)
- **Border / Rule:** `#E5E1D8`
- **Typography:** Modern clean sans-serif (Inter / System Sans)

## Audio Direction
- **Role:** Gentle, warm ambient bed that complements the skincare aesthetic without overpowering the text.
- **Track:** `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
  - **Tempo:** ~114 BPM (gentle, warm acoustic/electronic blend)
  - **Volume:** -18 dB to -20 dB background level
  - **Fades:** 2.0-second fade-in at start; 3.0-second fade-out ending at 1:20.
- **SFX (Subtle & Quiet):**
  - Message send: Soft mechanical tick (`ui/click1.ogg`) at -22 dB.
  - Bot reply arrival: Gentle interface chime (`interface/bong_001.ogg`) at -24 dB.
  - Section change: Subtle swoosh/switch (`interface/switch_001.ogg`) at -20 dB.

## Drop-In Instructions for User's Screen Recording Clip

- File name: `chat-clip.mp4`
- Location: Place inside `brag-output/assets/chat-clip.mp4`
