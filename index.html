Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our orders, shipping, and skincare compatibility.

We offer Free Standard Shipping on all orders over $50 within the contiguous United States. For orders under $50, standard shipping is a flat rate of $5. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping is available for $15 and delivers within 1 to 2 business days. All orders are processed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours on business days.

We stand behind our formulas with a 30-day return policy. You may return any unopened products in original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Additionally, if an opened product caused an adverse skin reaction or irritation, you are also eligible for a full refund or exchange within 30 days. To initiate a return, contact our support team with your order number and photo or brief description of the reaction. Once received at our facility, refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Yes, 100%. Veloura Skin is proud to be Leaping Bunny certified cruelty-free. We never test our formulas or ingredients on animals at any stage of development, nor do we sell in markets that require animal testing. Every product in our collection is 100% vegan, containing no animal byproducts or animal-derived ingredients (our squalane is 100% plant-derived from sugarcane).

We always recommend patch testing before introducing any new skincare product to your daily regimen. Apply a pea-sized amount to a discreet area such as the inside of your upper forearm or behind your ear. Leave it untouched for 24 hours and observe for any redness, itching, burning, or irritation. If no reaction occurs, you may safely introduce the product to your face. If mild irritation develops, rinse with water and discontinue use.

We recommend separating Vitamin C and Retinol into opposite routines to maximize efficacy and prevent barrier sensitization. Use Glow Vitamin C Serum in the morning on cleansed skin to provide antioxidant protection against daytime environmental stressors, followed by sunscreen. Use Renew Retinol 0.3% Night Serum exclusively in the evening to stimulate overnight cellular renewal. Never combine Retinol and Vitamin C or exfoliating acids in the exact same application step.

Our Renew Retinol 0.3% Night Serum contains pure encapsulated vitamin A and should be avoided if you are pregnant, nursing, or undergoing prescription dermatological treatments unless cleared by your physician. For sensitive or reactive skin flares, Gentle Cloud Cleanser, Hydra Hyaluronic Serum, Cloud Barrier Cream, and Mineral Sheer SPF 30 are our most comforting, barrier-rebuilding recommendations.
