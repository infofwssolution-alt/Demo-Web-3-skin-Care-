Customer Care & Routine Guidance

What are your shipping rates and delivery times?

We offer Free Standard Shipping on all orders over $50 within the contiguous United States. For orders under $50, standard shipping is a flat rate of $5. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping is available for $15 and delivers within 1 to 2 business days. All orders are processed within 24 to 48 hours on business days.

What is your return policy on opened products?

You may return any unopened products in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. In addition, if an opened product caused an adverse skin reaction or irritation, you are also eligible for a full refund or exchange within 30 days. Contact support with your order number and reaction details for a prepaid return label. Refunds process within 5 to 7 business days.

Can I use Vitamin C and Retinol together?

We recommend separating them into opposite times of day to prevent barrier irritation. Apply Glow Vitamin C Serum in the morning on cleansed skin, followed by sunscreen (Daily Shield SPF 50). Apply Renew Retinol 0.3% Night Serum exclusively in the evening. Never combine them in the same application step.

Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes, 100%. We are Leaping Bunny certified cruelty-free. We never test on animals at any stage of development. Every formulation is 100% vegan, with plant-derived squalane from sugarcane and zero animal byproducts.

How should I patch test new active formulas?

Apply a pea-sized amount behind your ear or on the inner forearm and leave it for 24 hours. If redness, burning, itching, or swelling occurs, do not apply the product to your face.
